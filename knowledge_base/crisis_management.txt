CRISIS MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. A demand shock represents a sudden increase in customer demand.

2. During a demand shock, predicted demand should be adjusted before inventory and procurement decisions are made.

3. A supplier delay increases the effective replenishment lead time and may increase inventory risk.

4. During supplier disruption, available suppliers should be reevaluated using their current cost, lead time, and reliability.

5. A supplier cost increase should trigger a comparison of available supplier options.

6. A combined crisis may involve multiple simultaneous changes, such as increased demand, supplier delays, and cost increases.

7. During a combined crisis, inventory requirements and supplier conditions should be evaluated together.

8. Crisis recommendations generated by the simulation are decision-support outputs and should not be treated as automatic real-world procurement orders.

9. The AI assistant should clearly state the assumptions and simulated nature of crisis scenarios.

10. The system should not invent external events or supplier information that is not present in the simulation data.
