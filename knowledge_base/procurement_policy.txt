SUPPLY CHAIN PROCUREMENT POLICY

1. Procurement decisions should consider supplier cost, lead time, and reliability.

2. A supplier with a lower cost is not automatically the preferred supplier. Reliability and delivery time should also be considered.

3. When inventory risk is high, the recommended order quantity should be reviewed carefully before procurement approval.

4. Supplier selection should consider current supply-chain conditions and any active disruption.

5. During a supplier delay, alternative suppliers should be evaluated based on their cost, lead time, and reliability.

6. During a demand increase, inventory requirements should be reassessed before placing an order.

7. During a cost increase, the system should compare available suppliers rather than relying only on the previous supplier selection.

8. All procurement recommendations generated by the simulation are decision-support recommendations and should be reviewed by a human before real-world purchasing.

9. Supplier information used by the system should come from the available supplier dataset. The AI assistant should not invent supplier information.

10. The AI assistant should clearly distinguish between simulated recommendations and real procurement decisions.
