SUPPLIER EVALUATION POLICY

1. Supplier evaluation should consider unit cost, lead time, and reliability.

2. Unit cost represents the estimated cost per unit supplied.

3. Lead time represents the expected number of days required for delivery.

4. Reliability represents the expected consistency of supplier performance.

5. A supplier with a higher reliability score may be preferable when supply disruption risk is important.

6. A shorter lead time can reduce the risk associated with delayed replenishment.

7. Supplier risk should be evaluated using the supplier information available in the simulation.

8. During a crisis scenario, supplier conditions may change and the system should reevaluate supplier risk.

9. The AI assistant must not create or assume supplier characteristics that are not present in the available data.

10. Final supplier decisions generated by the simulation are recommendations and should be reviewed by a human before real-world procurement.
