INVENTORY MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. Inventory planning should consider predicted demand, current inventory, safety stock, and lead-time demand.

2. Reorder point represents the inventory level at which replenishment should be considered.

3. Safety stock provides additional inventory protection against demand variability and supply uncertainty.

4. When stockout risk is high, inventory should be reviewed before procurement approval.

5. Higher predicted demand may require a higher replenishment quantity.

6. Longer supplier lead times can increase inventory requirements because replenishment takes more time.

7. Inventory recommendations generated by the system are based on simulated data and model calculations.

8. The AI assistant must use the inventory values produced by the system and must not invent inventory figures.

9. Inventory recommendations should be reviewed by a human before being used for actual business decisions.
